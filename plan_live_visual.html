How your live CRM works

Vercel serves the website and app. Your VPS handles Meta, shared CRM records, and live updates. This walkthrough illustrates the implemented device-history flow and optional assistant; it sends no data. Retained shared history remains a proposed alternative.

Always shared through the database

Contacts, client details, notes, assignments, statuses, consent, and business settings. Access is checked for each tenant, branch, user, and sender.

Personal browser state

Preferences belong in localStorage. Chat caches belong in IndexedDB. Proposed login credentials use a secure HttpOnly refresh cookie and an in-memory access token.
